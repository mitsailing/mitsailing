# MIT Sailing Product Context

register: product

## Product

MIT Sailing is the public and operational web app for the MIT Sailing Pavilion. It helps students, MIT community members, alumni, affiliates, guests, and staff find sailing information, register for events and classes, reserve Pavilion spaces, manage sailing cards, and operate admin workflows.

## Primary users

- New sailors trying to get eligible and take their first class.
- Returning sailors checking classes, events, ratings, cards, and Pavilion information.
- MIT Sailing staff and volunteers handling operational admin work.
- Public visitors learning whether MIT Sailing is open to them.

## Core jobs

- Make eligibility, membership, class registration, and sailing card requirements understandable.
- Let users complete account, onboarding, registration, and reservation tasks with low friction.
- Give staff dense, reliable admin tools for repeated operational work.
- Preserve the MIT Sailing identity while keeping product surfaces quiet and task-focused.

## Voice

Clear, direct, and practical. Use plain language, sentence case, and short help text. Explain requirements only when they change what the user should do next. Avoid marketing filler, jokes, legal-sounding hedges, and repeated explanations.

## Experience principles

- Progressive disclosure beats long forms. Ask only what is needed now.
- Requirements should be paired with the next action, not left as dead ends.
- Public and admin pages should feel related, but admin screens can be denser.
- Membership, payment, and eligibility copy must be precise, current, and easy to scan.
- Accessibility and i18n are product requirements, not polish.

## Anti-patterns

- Landing-page styling on task flows.
- Dense explanatory cards when a label or one sentence is enough.
- Hidden requirements that appear only after submit.
- Raw colors, hard-coded visible strings, and one-off controls when shared patterns exist.
- Implementing payment or external signup automation before the integration is explicitly scoped.
